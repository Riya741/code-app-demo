## What the data led to
- **This run measured a prompt change.** The first live draft (AIB-T07) was hard to read: 441 words cut to 350,
  long citation chains, and IDs written in the text.
  - CR-20261124-1 introduced Writer prompt v2 and a 3-marker cap in the Guard.
  - On this run: 26–318 words (mean 228), none over budget, at most 3 IDs per sentence, and one sentence still had IDs
    in its text.
- **Recall is the open quality issue.** 5 of 8 cases leave out a must-mention fact (`missing_mention_cases` 5). That
  metric has no threshold yet. Adding one is an owner decision and needs a CR; it is recorded in the AIB-T11 hand-off.
- **Measured cost per case is $0.043.** That is what `evals/README.md` uses to project the full run: about $0.13 for
  the 3-case pilot and $1.40 for all 32 cases.
